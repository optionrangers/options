Before an option buy

Three things to check, every time

CHECK 1

Direction & momentum

Is price actually moving in the direction you expect, or are you hoping it will?

CHECK 2

Time & theta

Options have an expiry. Time value erodes every day, even if price stays still.

CHECK 3

Strike & risk

Check strike distance, premium, liquidity, and the loss you can actually afford.

✓
DO

Trade with a plan

  • Wait for momentum to support your view.
  • Respect theta decay. Time works against the buyer.
  • Know your risk before entry, not after.
  • Check strike, expiry and liquidity.
  • Keep position size sensible.
×
DON'T

Common traps

  • Buy far OTM just because it looks cheap.
  • Average down blindly on a losing position.
  • Increase lots out of greed after a win.
  • Put your full capital into one expiry-day trade.
  • Enter without a reason you can write down.
